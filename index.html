STRATA is a camera that overlays ghost and outline guides from earlier photos so you can shoot the same frame again and compare changes over time. No account is required.

1. Information processed

  • Photos you take in the app, with capture time and a frame ID.
  • Outline and mask data calculated from each photo.
  • A random installation ID generated by the app. It is not an advertising ID, phone number or hardware serial number, and the server keeps only its hash.

Contacts, location, microphone and advertising data are not used.

2. Server-side image processing

Right after capture, the app sends the photo (JPEG, up to 1920 px wide) over HTTPS to the STRATA server. An image-processing algorithm on the server extracts the person outline and mask and returns them to the app. This server-side processing is a core function of the app. Photos received for this step are processed in memory; request bodies are not logged. Outline extraction is not facial recognition or biometric identification.

If the server is unreachable or no person is detected, the photo is saved without an outline and shown as a translucent ghost. An outline may be added later when the frame is synced.

3. Recovery sync and device storage

Each frame (photo, capture time, outline data and installation ID) is synced over HTTPS to private storage on the STRATA server so it can be restored with Recover from Server. Frames that cannot be synced stay on your device and are sent later. Frames are also kept in app storage, and a copy is saved to your photo gallery when permission allows. Recovery works by installation ID, so clearing app data, uninstalling or changing devices may prevent it.

4. Use and sharing

Data is used only for overlays, comparison, recovery and deletion requests. The app has no advertising or tracking, and photos are not sold or used for AI model training. The server is operated on hosting services whose providers may process IP addresses, access times and security logs.

5. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. Deleting a frame in the app removes it from the device and requests deletion of its server copy; if you are offline, the request is retried when connectivity returns. Deleted photos are permanently removed from the server within 7 days. Uninstalling alone does not delete server copies, and gallery copies must be deleted separately in your gallery. Provider security logs or disaster-recovery copies may follow separate retention cycles.

6. Permissions

Camera is used to take photos when you capture. Gallery access is used only to save copies; Android 13 and later do not need broad photo-library access. Permissions can be changed in your device settings.

7. Your requests

For access, correction or deletion requests, email youkim.official@gmail.com with the subject “STRATA data deletion request” and the minimum information needed to identify your records, such as capture dates. Requests are handled after verification, subject to applicable law.

8. Children and changes

Users must follow applicable minimum-age and parental-consent rules. Contact us if you believe a child's information was processed without required consent. Changes to this policy are reflected in its effective date.
