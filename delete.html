STRATA has no accounts. Your frames are linked to a random installation ID created by the app.

Delete in the app

Delete a frame in the app. This removes it from your device and requests deletion of its server copy. If you are offline, the request is retried when connectivity returns. Copies saved to your photo gallery must be deleted separately in your gallery.

Request deletion by email

To delete all of your data stored on the STRATA server, email youkim.official@gmail.com with the subject “STRATA data deletion request” and the minimum information needed to identify your records, such as capture dates. Requests are handled after verification, subject to applicable law.

What is deleted

Photos, capture times, outline and mask data and the installation ID hash stored on the server are deleted. Deleted photos are permanently removed from the server within 7 days. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete server copies. Hosting-provider security logs or disaster-recovery copies may follow separate retention cycles.
